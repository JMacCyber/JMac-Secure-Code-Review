# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not the person. Assume the other party is
trying to do good work.

## Expected

- Direct, specific, technical feedback. "This can be tricked by X" is good.
  "This is bad" is not feedback.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline something useful because it breaks
  a property the project exists to hold.
- Reporting security issues privately first, per `SECURITY.md`.
- Being accurate. Do not overstate what a change does, and do not overstate
  what a bug does.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately misrepresenting the security properties of this project, in
  either direction.
- Submitting code copied from another project as your own
  ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-clean-room-independent-implementation.md)).

## Scope

Everywhere the project exists — repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any space where you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Responses range from a private word, through a warning, to a
temporary or permanent ban, in proportion to what happened and whether it
continued after being raised.

Maintainers who do not hold themselves to this are subject to it too.
